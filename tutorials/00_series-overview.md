# Design Studio OS: AI Implementation Series
### A Video Tutorial Series by Design Thread Studio

---

## Series Purpose

Walk boutique interior designers step-by-step through implementing AI into their real workflows — not in theory, not in a corporate way, but practically and immediately. Each tutorial is 3–5 minutes and ends with one concrete action the viewer can take the same day.

---

## Target Viewer

A boutique interior designer who:
- Runs a 1–3 person studio
- Is curious about AI but hasn't committed to it yet (or started and stalled)
- Loses time to intake calls, proposal writing, client emails, and content creation
- Wants to feel elevated — not like a tech person — when using these tools

---

## Series Structure

| # | Title | Runtime | Core Outcome |
|---|-------|---------|--------------|
| 01 | Welcome to Your AI-Powered Studio | ~3 min | Mindset shift + series roadmap |
| 02 | The AI Client Intake System | ~5 min | Automated brief from a single form |
| 03 | Concept Development in Half the Time | ~5 min | AI-assisted mood board direction |
| 04 | Proposals That Write Themselves | ~5 min | First-draft proposal in 10 minutes |
| 05 | Client Communication on Autopilot | ~4 min | AI-generated updates, emails, check-ins |
| 06 | Turn Your Projects Into Content | ~4 min | Batch content creation from one project |

---

## Brand Voice Notes

- Warm, expert, unhurried — like a trusted colleague, not a tech tutorial channel
- Practical specificity: always give a real prompt, a real example, a real result
- Validate the overwhelm before solving it
- Reinforce: "You are still the designer. AI is your junior assistant."
