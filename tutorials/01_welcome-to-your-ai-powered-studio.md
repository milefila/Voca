## SCRIPT

**[OPEN ON: You at your desk, looking directly into camera. Natural, unhurried.]**

If you've been watching other designers talk about AI and thinking — "okay but how does that actually work *for me*, in a real project, with real clients" — this series is for you.

I'm [Name], and I run Design Thread Studio. Over the past year, I've been quietly building something I now call the Design Studio OS — a set of AI-powered systems that handle the parts of running a design business that used to eat my time and my energy.

Not the creative work. The *other* work.

The intake questionnaires. The first-draft proposals. The "just checking in" emails. The project timelines. The social media captions for a project you finished six months ago.

**[PAUSE. Small smile.]**

This series is six tutorials. Each one is focused on one specific part of your workflow, and each one ends with something you can actually do today — not someday.

Here's what we're covering:

**[TEXT ON SCREEN — each line appears as you say it:]**

- How to set up an AI intake system that gets you a client brief before you ever pick up the phone
- How to develop concepts faster without losing your design voice
- How to write proposals in a fraction of the time
- How to keep clients informed without writing every email from scratch
- And how to turn every finished project into weeks of social content

**[Back to camera, direct.]**

A few things I want you to understand before we start.

You are still the designer. AI doesn't have taste. It doesn't know your client's personality, or the way the afternoon light hits the east wall of their living room. That's yours. What AI can do is take everything you already know and help you communicate it faster, more clearly, and with less friction.

Think of it as a very well-read junior assistant who drafts things so you can decide — keep it, change it, or throw it out.

**[Slight lean in.]**

The other thing: you don't need to be technical. At all. You need to know how to type a sentence. That's the whole skill set.

Alright — let's build your AI-powered studio.

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: "Tutorial 02 → The AI Client Intake System"]**

Click the next video. See you there.
