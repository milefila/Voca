# Crosspost Task

## Session Goal
Adapt newsletter content for LinkedIn and Instagram.

## Checklist
- [ ] Read finalized newsletter draft
- [ ] Write LinkedIn post (300–700 words, professional, no hashtag spam)
- [ ] Write Instagram caption (hook + 3 bullets + soft CTA, under 150 words)
- [ ] Export both to Blotato for scheduling

## Platform Notes

### LinkedIn
- Open with a concrete result or counterintuitive statement
- Short paragraphs (1–2 sentences max)
- 2–3 relevant hashtags only, at the end
- No "I'm excited to share" openers

### Instagram
- Hook in first line (stops the scroll)
- Use line breaks liberally
- CTA in last line ("save this", "DM me X", "link in bio")
- 5–10 hashtags in first comment, not caption
