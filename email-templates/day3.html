If you've made it through Foundations and started Module 1, you've probably hit Lesson 1.2 — the Studio Voice Document.

This is the lesson most people skim. They read it, think "I'll do that later," and move on. They're making a mistake.

The Studio Voice Document is the single piece of the framework that makes everything else work. Without it, Claude produces good drafts. With it, Claude produces your drafts — the kind you'd send without editing.

It takes 20–30 minutes to write. The return is years of communication that doesn't require your voice to be manually present in every sentence.

"When you give Claude your voice document and a real scenario, the first draft often lands at 85% — not because Claude is magic, but because you've given it enough to work from. The remaining 15% is your edit. That ratio changes everything about how you spend your time."
